## Why does it matter?

<!-- AGENT:why-it-matters — Connect the work to specific goals, features, roadmap items, or known problems. Name them. -->

**D-009 was law and was being violated in production for 24 of 28 shots.** *No asset moves by row
number* has been on the books since D-009, and the shot registry enforced it at the workbook seam —
but `assets.json` bound by number underneath, and a number-bound registry does not fail loudly. It
silently hands the author the wrong character sheets. This is `GOALS.md` core problem 2 — *style
will not hold* — in its worst form: not drift the eye catches, but the wrong person's face arriving
correctly formatted. Every generation of shot 5 since the renumber was spending money on a prompt
built from the father's sheet. That is the first film-moving fix in this window and it is the
strongest thing in it.

**The continuity pass answers a structural objection, not a bug.** Narrow reviewers were a deliberate
design and the deliberateness is what hid the failure — every guard in the system had a one-shot
payload, so cross-shot disagreement had no observer by construction. Adding *one* wide-and-shallow
agent that reports and never resolves is the correct shape: it preserves product principle 1 (*the
pen stays in Vince's hand*) by refusing to arbitrate, and it fights drift rather than adding a
second place a fact can live. Core problem 4 — *workflows live in one person's head* — again, one
level up: "remember to compare shot 5 against shot 1" was a thing only Vince could hold.

**The PRD's §0 is the most valuable page written this week, and it is a refusal.** An agent handed a
draft that proposed four new models went and checked, found them already built, and rewrote the
document to say *surface, do not rebuild*. `GOALS.md` § *Keeping features tight* question 1 asks
whether a thing strengthens the single source of truth or creates a second one; §0 is that question
applied to a 636-line plan before a line of it shipped. Worth naming because the opposite behaviour —
building the plausible thing — is the default failure of a capable agent with a spec in hand.

**Boards went from 2 to 19.** Recommended action 2 of the 08-07 checkpoint was *get the boards onto
disk, then run one shot end to end*. The first half happened. `panel_reader.py` now has something to
read on two thirds of the film. The second half did not.

**And the governance layer still cannot record itself — day six.** The last checkpoint *committed to
git* is 2026-08-04. 08-05, 08-06, 08-07 and this one are untracked files on one disk, along with
their `PRODUCT_SPEC.md` / `ROADMAP.md` / `SKILL-INVENTORY.md` / D-020 / D-024 edits. The cause is
unchanged and mechanical — the Cowork mount denies `unlink`, so `.git/index.lock` survives and blocks
every write. `git status` printed the warning again this run. A checkpoint that reports on
everything except its own three-day-old failure is not reporting.
