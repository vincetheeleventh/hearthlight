## Why does it matter?

<!-- AGENT:why-it-matters — Connect the work to specific goals, features, roadmap items, or known problems. Name them. -->

**The densest statement of Vince's intent was invisible to the machine.** `GOALS.md` primary outcome
1 asks that the narrative material become documents *fetchable by agents*. A photographed board is
the most concrete thing Vince produces at Stage 4 and, until yesterday, no path existed from a panel
image to text. Product principle 1 — *the pen stays in Vince's hand* — was true in the gate protocol
and false in the data: his hand-drawn framing reached the prompt author only by his re-typing it.
`panel_reader.py` closes the gap that sits closest to his authorship.

**PANEL-READING.md is the sharper artifact, because it resists the obvious mistake.** A concrete
sketch outranking an abstract Vision would have quietly become the drawing dictating the film. TIER 3
baseline evidence, conflicts *reported and never resolved*, confidence required per observation, and
a warning when every reading returns 'high' — that is core problem 2 (*style will not hold*) handled
at the authority layer rather than one prompt at a time.

**The invisible-contract discovery is a failure class, not an incident.** `PROMPT-AUTHOR.md` was
built, correct, and about to be rebuilt from scratch by the next agent through it. That is core
problem 4 — *workflows live in one person's head* — reappearing one level up: a systematized workflow
nothing points at is functionally unsystematized. The four-place fix and the selfcheck RED treat it
as a class. Worth watching whether four pointers become four places to drift.

**`shots.json` canonical gives D-009 a home.** *No asset moves by row number* has been law since
D-009, but a spreadsheet that both fed and recorded the film was the exact habitat for the failure.
`ROADMAP.md` limitation 10 — *the image ledger is write-only* — is the same wound; the workbook
becoming a one-directional export removes the bidirectional seam that let the two diverge.

**And the thing that did not happen: the film did not move.** No still was approved, no clip
generated. That is two consecutive days where the prior question in `GOALS.md` was answered by the
second half — *harden the pipeline carrying it* — and not the first.
