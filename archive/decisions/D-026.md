# D-026 — No gates. Approval is per shot.

**Decided 2026-08-11 by Vince.** Supersedes D-007.

## Context

The six-gate protocol was the most-repeated law in the system. It appears in `CLAUDE.md`,
`AGENTS.md`, `profile/SOUL.md`, `PRODUCT_SPEC.md`, `GOALS.md`, `USER-GUIDE.md`, 41 skill files and
8 scripts. *"Gates are sacred — nothing advances without Vince's explicit ✅"* was stated more times
than any other rule in Hearthlight.

It was also the one rule not being followed. On 2026-08-11, `projects/yugioh/status.yml` read:

```
distribution_spec: pending      gate2_mise_en_scene: pending
gate0_vision:      pending      gate3_images:        pending
gate1_outline:     pending      gate4_storyboard:    approved 2026-07-30
critique:          pending      gate5_video:         pending
```

One gate approved out of ten, against **40 completed generations** and a film at the conditioning-
still stage. Two proposals had been open for over a week (P-004, P-013) trying to reconcile the
ledger with reality, and the second of them existed only because no autonomy tier was allowed to
write the fix.

## Reason

**A gate is a team instrument.** It exists so that a crew of forty does not run ahead of the
director — so that layout does not start before story is locked, because forty people moving in the
wrong direction is expensive. Hearthlight has one operator. Vince is the director *and* the crew.
Most of the gates were him asking himself for permission, and he correctly ignored them.

The cost of keeping a ceremony nobody performs is not neutral. It:

- made the system's central promise a fiction, which devalues every other law beside it;
- produced a machine-readable progress record that was **wrong**, and that downstream judgments
  (checkpoints, alignment reports, video prompt packets for shots 2 and 8) were reading as truth;
- generated governance work — two proposals, a tier redesign — to service the fiction rather than
  the film.

The approval that *does* happen is per shot, and it was already modelled: `Design` and `Production`
run as independent axes on the shot record and in the Studio UI, so `Design: LOCKED · Production:
NEEDS FIX` says "fix the image, not the shot." That is the real unit of Vince's judgment. The film
does not advance in six steps; it advances one shot at a time, and shots do not advance in lockstep.

## What survives

The protection D-007 actually bought is **not** the ceremony. It is: *the machine never approves its
own work.* That is kept, and stated without gates:

- Agents draft, run and report. Only Vince marks a design **Locked** or a shot **Approved**.
- Stage A / Stage B in `hearthlight-shot-runner` is unchanged — spec compliance is machine-judged,
  quality is Vince only. That was never a gate; it is a review split.
- `Inputs: Broken` still blocks generation. A machine-computed correctness check is not an approval,
  and it is the one block that should be automatic.

## Consequences

- `status.yml` retires as a gate ledger. Per-shot state lives in the shot record.
- P-004 and P-013 are closed as moot — they existed to correct a ledger that no longer exists.
- `hearthlight-dashboard` loses the gate-ledger contract and reports shot state instead.
- The Studio's gate rail is replaced by a count of shots per production state.
- Removal is staged, not atomic: the runbook is `governance/GATE-REMOVAL.md`.

## The risk, stated plainly

Gates were also a **pacing** device — they forced a stop-and-look before spending. Removing them
removes a brake. The replacement brake is per-shot: nothing is Approved except by Vince, and
`Inputs: Broken` blocks generation. If work starts running ahead of judgment again, the answer is
not to reinstate ten gates; it is to make the per-shot state harder to skip.
