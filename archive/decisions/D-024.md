# D-024 · `shots.json` is the shot record; the workbook is a one-way export

**Date:** 2026-08-06 · **Source:** the change itself · **Agent:** cowork
**Recorded:** 2026-08-07 by the daily checkpoint — descriptive of a rule already in force in code.
**⚠️ Not yet ratified by Vince.** Written down because it binds behaviour and was undocumented.

**Context.** The shot-list spreadsheet both fed the pipeline and recorded its results. Two systems
wrote to the same facts, so the same fact could be stated in two places and diverge — which is the
habitat D-009 (*no asset moves by row number*) exists to drain, and the same wound `ROADMAP.md`
limitation 10 names as *the image ledger is write-only*. A hand-drawn panel pasted into a spreadsheet
cell was also, in practice, unreadable: no path existed from a workbook image to a file an agent
could open.

**Decision.** `projects/{slug}/05-storyboard/shots.json` is the canonical shot record. It carries
`prompt.still`, `prompt.action`, and `panel.path` per shot. Panels are **files on disk** at
`storyboard-panels/shot-{nn}-board.png`, found by lookup and never by guessing a filename. Edits go
through `shot_record.py`, which logs the revision actually applied to `shot-edits.jsonl`. The
workbook is regenerated from the record by `export_shotlist.py` and **nothing in production reads
it** — it is a handoff artifact for humans and for tools outside Hearthlight.

**Consequences.**
- Compilers read the record, not the spreadsheet. `krea_style_comp.py` already does.
- `hearthlight-selfcheck` WARNs when a project still depends on the workbook.
- Re-pasting a workbook cannot silently overwrite Studio state; the import route is
  `workflows/board-intake.md`, which is idempotent.
- The panel becomes machine-readable, which is what `panel_reader.py` and
  `hearthlight-image-prompts/references/PANEL-READING.md` are built on. That contract — a panel is
  TIER 3 baseline evidence, never above the current Shot Vision — is a **separate** rule about
  creative authority and is deliberately not folded in here. It wants its own entry from Vince.

**The alternative this closes.** Keeping the workbook editable as a second write surface, on the
grounds that a spreadsheet is where Vince actually works. Rejected: convenience at the input layer
bought a divergence risk at the identity layer. The export keeps the convenience without the risk.
