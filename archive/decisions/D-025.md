# D-025 — Hearthlight is a tool, not an engine underneath a product

**Decided 2026-08-11 by Vince.** Supersedes the framing in D-003; keeps its rule.

## What changed

Vince rewrote `PRODUCT_SPEC.md` § *What the product is*:

> Hearthlight is a **single-operator ai filmmaking tool** that makes it easy to go from story vision
> to ai-generated image and video assets. It helps organize and transform the director's input
> (storyboards, verbal expression of vision, narrative beats, etc) into a format that's ready for ai
> generation (prompts, generation settings, etc).
>
> This is combined with "Film Study Tool" user interface to be a visual shot tracker and production
> dashboard. The user iterates on their film using Hearthlight, so It's secondary role is visualize
> the current state of the film.
>
> Hearthlight itself is being iterated on as the user makes films, (improving the agent instructions
> and skills, adding features to reduce friction points, etc) improving the user-hearthlight dynamic.

Canon said the opposite in five places, most bluntly `AGENTS.md`: *"Hearthlight is the ENGINE, not a
product."* Also `CLAUDE.md`, `GOALS.md`, `README.md`, and D-003 itself.

## Why the framing went

The engine/client split was invented to solve a real problem: Talefeather's grief register was
leaking into projects that had nothing to do with grief. Calling Hearthlight an engine and
Talefeather a client running on it made the leak describable, and the fix stuck.

But the metaphor did more work than it was asked to. "Engine, not a product" implied Hearthlight had
no user-facing identity of its own — which stopped being true the moment the Film Study Tool UI
became Vince's primary point of contact, and was never true of the thing he actually uses every day.

## What survives

The rule, restated without the metaphor: **nothing about a project is assumed.** `format`, `client`
and `charged_register` are declared in `projects/{slug}/distribution-spec.md` and obeyed. Guessing
one is a defect, and defaulting to grief is the specific defect this was written for. `client: none`
is the normal value.

Talefeather is still a client, still at `profile/clients/talefeather/`, still loaded only when a
project asks for it. Nothing about the guard weakened; only the identity claim was dropped.

## Consequences

- `HANDOFF.md` and root `AUDIENCE-CONTEXT.md` were archived the same day (P-006). Both stated the
  engine framing, and `HANDOFF.md` stated Talefeather's grief reasoning as though it applied to every
  project — the exact leak D-003 existed to prevent.
- The Film Study Tool UI is named in the product definition rather than treated as an adjacent
  utility.
- Self-improvement — the instruction layer getting better as films get made — is part of what the
  product *is*, not just something that happens to it.
