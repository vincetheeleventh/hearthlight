# TASTE — Vince's aesthetic memory (cross-project)

The thing that turns Hearthlight from a populator into a partner: a persistent record of HOW Vince
thinks about film — what he kills, what he reaches for, his rules of thumb. **Every project reads
this. Every rejection/decision updates it.** Read it in `hearthlight-critique`, `hearthlight-outline`,
and `hearthlight-mise-en-scene` so proposals already sound like him.

This is taste, not story. It carries across projects (the McConaughey pilot's specifics live in
that project's docs, not here). When in doubt, propose in the direction these verdicts point.

---

## Reaches for (lean THIS way)
- **Restraint over sentiment.** The quiet release lands harder than the swell. Stillness after motion.
- **The held wide / the withheld reaction.** Hide the most important moment to make it bigger (Pop's face withheld; cutaway during the decision; the from-window jump seen from a distance).
- **Counterpoint, not echo.** The image does something other than illustrate the words.
- **Charged objects that *change*** across a scene (the coiled cord: taut → slack at the blessing). Motifs that resolve, not just appear.
- **Composition as argument** (small-in-frame = trapped; same frame inverted = private joy). The contrast spine made visual.
- **The real voice as the hero** — never generated audio. Silence as an instrument.
- **Specificity as feeling** — one small true moment over a montage of bigness.

## Kills (do NOT propose these)
- **Montage-of-success endings** (e.g. the film-roles montage). Cheapens the moment; answers a question the emotional story didn't ask. End on the human, not the fame.
- **Sentimentality reaching for tears the audience hasn't earned.** No string-section swell under the build.
- **Echo shots** (image literally illustrating the VO).
- **Close-up inflation** — spending CUs so freely the important ones lose force.
- **The obvious beat as the ONLY version** (a bare jump-for-joy in close-up) — it must be surrounded or earned.
- **Generated/AI-look images** where the brief wants the human hand. ("NO AI-look images.")
- **The literal/cute version** (a calendar with the day circled) over the felt version (the churn settling).

## Rules of thumb (his working principles, observed)
- **Cold open on the loudest line, then rewind to build.**
- **Break the cut rhythm on a turn** — if it's been wide/close/wide/close, the decision beat gets a *third* register (the still medium).
- **Two stillnesses, opposite emotions** — active resolve vs passive dread — stage as a matched pair.
- **Hold a shot a touch too long** to let the ache underneath the joy in.
- **The blessing is two gifts** — permission AND love — worth landing separately.
- **Aspect ratio is a composition law, not a crop.** Decide format first.

## How to update this file
When Vince rejects a proposed shot/ending/beat, or strongly endorses one, add a one-line verdict
here (under Reaches-for / Kills / Rules). Keep it terse and general — taste, not project trivia.
Propose the addition to Vince; he confirms. Over ten projects this becomes a model of his eye.
