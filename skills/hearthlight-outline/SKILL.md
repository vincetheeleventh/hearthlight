# Hearthlight — Outline Documents (Stage 2)

## When to Use
After Vince confirms the Vision Brief as binding source material.

## The authorship law
Stage 2 is **expansion of the Vision Brief, not invention beyond it.** This is where authorship could quietly leak from Vince to the machine. The machine drafts the skeleton; Vince places the heart:
- Structure, ordering, pacing proposals: yours to draft.
- The charged devices — the withheld turn, the detonation image, what the payoff moment IS — are **open creative slots**. Where the structure needs one, write `[OPEN SLOT: the image we hold on when the blessing lands — yours]` and flag it in the Telegram post. Never fill a slot with a plausible default.
- Flag structural gaps as observations ("there is no image for the moment before he dials"), phrased as questions, not patches.

## The three documents (written in order, each building on the last)

### 1. `02-outline/story-arc.md` — the emotional spine, one page
- What the moment IS (one sentence, anchored in the Vision Brief's arc statement).
- What turns (fear → blessing), and where the charge lives.
- ONE designated **detonation beat** — the single moment the whole piece exists to deliver. If the Brief doesn't name it, that's an open slot, not a choice you make.

Each beat's headline doubles as the future shot's `one_liner` (the narrative sidecar,
`05-storyboard/shot-narrative.json`) — write it as the sentence the eventual shot must answer to,
not a content label. It travels downstream into the shot registry's narrative layer.

### 2. `02-outline/beat-sheet.md` — numbered beats
Per beat: **(a)** what happens, **(b)** what we SEE — which must counterpoint, not echo, what we hear, **(c)** emotional temperature (one word), **(d)** which VO segment it maps to (timestamps from `01-intake/segment-timestamps.md`).

**Counterpoint, not echo.** Words and pictures must not say the same thing. VO says "I was terrified" → we see stillness: a hand flat on the glass, traffic sliding past unconcerned. If a beat's image merely illustrates its line, redo it or flag it.

**Withhold the payoff.** The emotional payoff word/image arrives at the turn, not before. Don't spend the blessing early.

**Beat count:** ~1 image per 4–8 seconds of VO as the starting heuristic. A 90-second segment → roughly 12–20 beats; pilot targets 8–12, so propose cuts if over.

### 3. `02-outline/av-script.md` — two columns
Left: AUDIO — VO segment text with timestamps, ambient/sound notes. Right: VISUAL — one row per planned image, carrying the beat number, frozen-moment description, and a VO-density note (dense VO → flag for negative-space composition at Stage 4).

## Procedure
1. Read the Vision Brief (contract), rant transcript (voice), interview transcript + segment timestamps (raw material).
2. Draft the three docs in order. Trace every content claim to the Brief or the interview; structural craft is yours, content is not.
3. Post to Telegram one document at a time (arc first — if the spine is wrong, beats are wasted work). List all open slots and gap observations at the top of each post.
4. Vince approves, edits, or sends voice-note revisions; voice notes are transcribed and applied, with changes named back to him (playback check).
5. **Review:** Vince confirms or revises each document. Confirmed material becomes binding; unresolved items stay explicit.

## Pitfalls
- Filling an open slot because the draft "needed an ending". The dove-level inventions are his.
- Echo beats (image illustrating the words).
- Inventing biographical details not in the interview or Brief ("his father paused" — did he? cite or cut).
- Beat counts that balloon past what the VO can carry.
- Posting all three docs at once and getting a blurry blanket approval — one at a time, arc first.

## Verification
- Every beat's content traces to Brief or interview; every beat maps to a VO timestamp.
- Open slots are marked and listed, none silently filled.
- Each doc records its current confirmation state without implying that unrelated work must stop.
